🐒 Jungle Monkey 3D

Your hand is the controller. Eat only bananas. Other fruits cost a life!

  • 🖐 Open hand → the monkey runs
  • ✊ Make a fist → the monkey stops
  • 🤏 Pinch (thumb + index) or flick hand up → jump over logs
  • ↔ Move your hand left / centre / right → switch lanes

Hold one hand up in front of the camera in good light. Each banana = 105 kcal.
No camera? Keys: ↑/W run, Space jump, ←/→ lanes.
